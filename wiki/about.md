# About — AI for Shlichus (Shluchim)

A WhatsApp group started on **2025/07/28**, gathering Chabad-Lubavitch
*shluchim* (emissaries) — and shluchos, mashpiim, mosdos administrators,
designers, developers, and a handful of philanthropists — around the
practical question:

> *What can AI actually do for shlichus work?*

By **2025/11/13** (the eve of Kinus Hashluchim) the group's join-link was
filled by 100+ requests in a few hours, and the chat became the largest
day-to-day forum where Chabad's frontline workers compare prompts,
critique each other's flyers, ship apps over Yomim Tovim, and argue about
whether Claude or Gemini handles a *maamar* better. By **2026/05** it had
spawned a separate **AIFS Builders** group for vibe-coders, a **Shluchos**
group for women emissaries, a webinar series, and dozens of community-built
tools.

## What's in the chat

About **7,431 messages** (~1 MB of text) from ~60 active participants and
~200 total members, between **2025/07/28** and **2026/05/25**. Voice notes,
images, and PDFs are not in the export, only the text and the URLs.

## What the group is *for*

In the participants' own words, repeatedly:

- **"Real ways I've used AI in shlichus."** Not theory, not benchmarks —
  what shipped, what failed, what saved an hour.
- **Pressure-testing.** "Ideas get pressure-tested, iterated, and deployed
  into real tools" (The Farbrengen Podcast, [chat 2026-04-23]).
- **Mutual aid for non-technical shluchim** standing up flyers, CRMs,
  websites, and curricula with AI.
- **Hashkafic clarity.** What does it mean when *Bina Melachutit*
  (artificial intelligence) has *bina* but no *chochma*? Can a sicha be
  AI-generated? Can an agent run on Shabbos?
