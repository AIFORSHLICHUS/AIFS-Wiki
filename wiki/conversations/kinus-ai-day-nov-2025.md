# Kinus Hashluchim AI Day — November 2025

The pivotal community event in the chat's first season. Crystallized
the group from "WhatsApp curiosity channel" into an organized
ecosystem with shared infrastructure (ShluchimExchange.ai), shared
vocabulary, and a much larger membership.

## Setup

- **Where**: Kinus Hashluchim, Brooklyn, **2025-11-13** to 11-14.
- **Format**: Full AI track / Thursday "AI Day" with workshops, product
  launches, panel of shluchim sharing success stories.
- **Speakers / contributors mentioned**: Chabad shluchim, AI
  practitioners, and a Bar-Ilan professor presenting on rabbinic AI.

## What launched

- **[[../tools/shluchimexchange-ai]]** — *"NEW (Nov 13, 2025).
  Platform for Shluchim to share effective AI prompts and workflows.
  Beta signup available."*
- **[[../tools/dicta-mekorotai]]** — *"demoed at Kinus yesterday will
  be out this year some time."* [chat 2025-11-14]
  Backed by Claude API. Built by the Bar-Ilan team.
- **Nano Banana 2** — coincided with Google's Gemini 3 Pro release
  on **2025-11-20**, lighting up the chat. (Officially launched by
  Google rather than at Kinus, but the timing reframed every flyer
  discussion.)

## Membership surge

The group ballooned. [chat 2025-11-13] *"70+ joined in last day"*,
and 100+ requests came in within hours of the public announcement
of the AI Day.

## What people brought home

- **A shared mental model.** Bi-weekly recap posts began after this —
  first one [chat 2025-11-18]. That became the cadence the group runs on.
- **A community platform.** ShluchimExchange.ai started filling with
  agents, prompts, and remixable workflows.
- **A vision for AI Torah accessibility.** [chat 2026-03-23]:
  > *"Yesterday we had a very lively conversation on the builders
  > group about making sifrei chassidus accessible to use in our apps
  > and tools. It's clearly a very important issue and a key to
  > unblocking the next frontier in hafotzas hamaayanos. 'Sefaria for
  > chassidus.'"*

## The famous (half-joking) line

[chat 2025-11-14]:

> *"Everyone in the world is going to lose their jobs besides for
> shluchim."*

[chat 2025-11-14]:

> *"Torah AI will educate your mekuravim … make you באין ערוך more
> in demand."*

This became the chat's working *hashkafa* — AI as a tool that
*expands* the value of human shluchim rather than replacing them.
