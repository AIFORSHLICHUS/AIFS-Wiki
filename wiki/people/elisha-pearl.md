# Elisha Pearl

The community's technical-depth voice. Runs model bake-offs, explains
LLM internals, and pushes the group's thinking on AI's limits for
Torah work.

## Areas of expertise

- **Model comparison.** Has tested Claude, GPT-5, Gemini 2.5, Grok,
  and others extensively on Chassidus tasks, Torah questions, and
  code. His verdicts are usually quoted as group consensus.
- **Speech-to-text.** Knows the Whisper / Parakeet / 11Labs landscape
  cold; recommended Whisper as "the best" with detailed reasons.
  [chat 2025-08-16]
- **AI infrastructure.** API usage, token costs, when to use Claude
  API directly vs. chat app.
- **Dicta / Mekorotai project.** Closest to the team from the AIFS
  side; primary commentator on its progress. See
  [[../tools/dicta-mekorotai]].

## Editorial positions

> *"Claude is better at coding and iyyun. Gemini is more fun, quicker
> and cheaper if technical accuracy is less important."*
> — [chat 2025-08-13]

> *"Use Claude for technical/deep work. Gemini for quick/fun popular
> content. GPT for personality. Always test yourself."*
> — synthesized 2025-08

> *"Without structured data — all of Igros indexed — this problem will
> persist for years. Unless OpenAI/Anthropic gives access to all texts
