# Mendel Super

The chat's principled skeptic. The voice that keeps the AI hype
honest, especially on Torah and design.

## Editorial positions

> *"AI has very little access to actual Torah sources. It's skimming
> basic English sites … regurgitating chabad.org back at you."*
> — [chat 2026-02-24]

> *"Telltale AI flyer. The design is busy and all over the place. It
> might be easy to produce, but it's not good graphics. The same way
> AI text is horrible at replacing human writing, AI graphics should
> still not be considered a replacement for a human graphic artist."*
> — [chat 2026-02-11]

> *"A non-patronizing AI. 'That's a great idea! You're brilliant!'
> You're a robot. Stop it."*
> — [chat 2026-04-16]

> *"Gemini recently took a turn for the worse. Giving bad information
> regularly."*
> — [chat 2026-04-21]

> *"Too difficult to teach it Russian Yiddish. Not enough samples
> available."*
> — on AI for Rebbe's weekday sichos transcription, [chat 2025-12-12]

> *"Needs English side-by-side. So it scrolls the English together
> with the Hebrew."*
> — [chat 2026-02-22], on the early Megillah app.

## Areas where his skepticism shows up

- **AI-generated divrei Torah** lack the *Divine unity* of human chiddush.
- **AI flyer aesthetics** aren't a substitute for a human designer.
- **AI sycophancy** is a tell, not a feature.
- **Gemini quality** is volatile and currently regressing.
- **Yiddish/Russian Yiddish** is too data-sparse for AI to handle well.
- **Vibe-coded systems** without professional oversight are risky.

## Style

Sharp, terse, repeatedly *correct in retrospect*. Calls out
patterns the rest of the chat eventually has to acknowledge. Not
anti-AI — anti-overclaim.
