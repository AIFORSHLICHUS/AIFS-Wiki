# Mendy Elishevitz

Builder of [[../tools/megillah-app]], **mikdash.live**, **maamorim.app**,
**mishna.me**, and the in-progress **vertlach.net**. The most prolific
shipper of Chabad-specific apps in the chat.

## Things he's built

- **[[../tools/megillah-app]]** — synchronized Megillah reading with
  broadcast mode. Built in ~10 days with live community feedback over
  Purim 2026. Open-source at `github.com/kadmonim/megillah.app`.
- **Mishna.me** — shareable links for learning mishnayos with *osiyos
  hashem* for shiva / yahrtzeits. [chat 2026-03-22]
- **[[../tools/maamorim-app]]** — curated Rebbe's maamorim with
  summaries by R' Shneur Zalman Farkash, AI search, favorites, notes.
- **mikdash.live** — interactive Beis Hamikdash portal (*"l'zchus
  Tucker Carlson"*) with scheduling for temple visits, offerings,
  purification ceremonies. [chat 2026-03, week review]
- **vertlach.net** — in development; Hebrew-only Torah learning tool
  analogous to ChatTorah.
- **Yiddish Labs** (`app.yiddishlabs.com`) — Yiddish audio transcribe,
  grammar-correct, rephrase, translate. [chat 2025-12-25]

## The megillah.app sprint

See [[../conversations/megillah-app-sprint-feb-2026]]. Mendy's
trademark: shipping v0 fast and merging community feedback within
hours. Built with [[../tools/claude]] (not Lovable). Took feature
requests on Hebrew font, transliteration, broadcast latency, kids
version, voice recognition, sound effects, language toggles. Most
were merged the same day.

## Style

Builder who listens. Long roadmap, tight feedback loop. Pushes back
politely when feature requests are technically out of reach (DND mode,
voice recognition). Open about which features he chose not to build.

## Areas of expertise

- **Full-stack web app development** with AI assistance.
- **Real-time synchronization** (websockets for megillah.app live mode).
- **Hebrew rendering** in web apps.
- **Rapid iteration from community feedback** — Mendy is the chat's
  best-known case study in "community + AI = ship fast."
