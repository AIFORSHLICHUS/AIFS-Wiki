# Mendy Shishler

Group creator and primary curator of **AI for Shlichus (Shluchim)**.
By message count, the most active contributor — 468 messages in the
first chunk alone, sustained throughout.

## Role

Founded the group on **2025-07-28** with the joke prompt: *"@Meta AI
can I programme you to give a weekly summary of all the messages in
this chat every week."* Has been doing essentially that job by hand
ever since:

- **Broadcasting major updates** — model releases (GPT 5.2, Gemini 3,
  Anthropic–SpaceX deal), feature drops (Magic Layers, Nano Banana
  Pro, Gemini Spark).
- **Running polls** to crystallize community sentiment (paid vs. free,
  model preferences, agent use cases).
- **Writing bi-weekly recap summaries.** [chat 2025-11-18] is the
  template — comprehensive synthesis of two weeks of scattered
  threads.
- **Convening webinars** (AI 101, then a vibe-coding follow-up) with
  Rabbi Zalman Abraham, 2026-03-16.
- **Spinning off subgroups** when noise demanded it — AIFS Builders
  (2026-02-22), AIFS for Shluchos / women's group.
- **Strategic vision** — proposed a seed fund for AI-for-Torah projects
  [chat 2026-03-10].

## Things he's built

- **SinaiGPT** — the original Chabad GPT, made *"ages ago"* and
  resurfaced 2025-07-30. `tinyurl.com/SinaiGPT`
- **Tenpr.app** (`tenpr.app`) — *maaser* education tool, built in Google
  AI Studio's vibe-coding canvas. [chat 2026-01-15]
- **The "7 AI Tells" Designer Prompt** — the chat's most-shared system
  prompt for human-designed flyers. See [[../resources/designer-prompt]].
- **The Replit AIFS workflows app** (`AIFS-workflows.replit.app/results`)
  for crowdsourcing use cases. [chat 2026-05-17]
