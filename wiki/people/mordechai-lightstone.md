# Mordechai Lightstone

The chat's image-generation strategist and Torah-AI conscience.
Writes a tech blog (*mytechtribe.org/dispatch*); regular voice on
prompt engineering and design ethics.

## Areas of expertise

- **Sora prompt engineering.** Co-developer (with
  [[rabbi-chaim-lazaroff]]) of the
  **clothing-and-context** prompt pattern that bypasses demographic
  refusals. See [[../tools/sora]] and
  [[../resources/master-prompts]].
- **Image-gen workflows** in general. AI prompt → article → image on
  Nano Banana/Midjourney → text in Canva.
- **Letter writing in Claude** — early advocate. *"Claude is better in
  my experience than ChatGPT. Provide a good outline of what you want
  the letter to say."* [chat 2025-08-04]
- **Sofer.ai fan** for transcription. [chat 2025-08-21]

## Things he's done / built

- **Shofar Sounds interactive game** built with Claude Artifacts.
  [chat 2025-09-01]
- **DIA Browser advocacy** — the writing-aware browser with built-in
  AI assistant where you pre-load samples and style. [chat 2025-08-04]
- **mytechtribe.org/dispatch** — personal tech blog.

## Notable warnings

- **Otter.ai contact-permission grab.** *"Otter AI will try to spam
  all of your contacts."* [chat 2025-11-17]
- **Shabbos candle imagery correction.** Three-candles rule for
  AI-generated candle-lighting flyers; the chat's reference voice for
  Chabad iconography fidelity.

## Editorial positions

> *"Once you specify hair visible, no head coverings, modern modest
> dresses, party clothes, etc., the model no longer 'plays it safe' by
> covering them with burkas."*
> — [chat 2025-11-20], the Sora prompt workaround.

> *"Chabad"* or *"Chasidic"* descriptors work better than *"modest"*
> (which triggers tichel/burka imagery).

> *"AI is ethical aid to writing/refinement, not source of original
> divrei Torah."*
> — paraphrasing Mordechai's position on the Posner devar-Torah-by-AI
> thread.

## Style

Calibrated; takes Torah seriously, takes design seriously, tests his
own claims. Frequent voice on image-generation iteration loops.
