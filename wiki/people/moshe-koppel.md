# Professor Moshe Koppel

Bar-Ilan computer scientist; major Hebrew NLP pioneer; serious
*baal nigleh* who teaches *Toras Ohr*. Lead figure behind
[[../tools/dicta-mekorotai]].

## Why the chat cares

- Speaker at **Kinus AI Day 2025-11-13**, billed as having *"major
  giluyim"* — revelations on AI and Chassidus.
- Heads the Dicta / Mekorotai project — the most ambitious shot at
  Torah-accurate AI in the chat's window.
- Bridges Bar-Ilan academic NLP with Chabad-flavored Torah scholarship.
- His group's pivot from academic Hebrew tools (Dicta the OG) to
  Chabad-specific *chabad.mekorotai.dicta.org.il* is what
  [[elisha-pearl]] tracks for the community.

## Status of his work in the chat

See [[../tools/dicta-mekorotai]] for the project page. As of
2026-02-17 — *"Not really working yet,"* per the chat. Still in
progress per Elisha Pearl.
