# Rayi Stern

The community's principal technical-strategist voice. Coordinates AI
sessions at Kinus, co-builds [[../tools/shluchimexchange-ai]],
champions vibe-coding *but* with safety guardrails.

## Areas of expertise

- **Vibe-coding patterns &amp; anti-patterns.** Rayi is the chat's
  loudest voice on safety: no logins, no payments, no databases, no
  mission-critical email in AI-built apps.
- **Meta-prompting** — "Have the AI Google best practices, rewrite the
  prompt in its own tone, test multiple times, have the AI grade the
  outputs and tweak accordingly. *You* tweak the prompt — that's the
  most important part." [chat 2026-02-16]
- **Tool curation** — video editing (CapCut, Descript, Filmora,
  Pictory, Gling, Clipchamp, VideoLeap), task management (Taskade,
  Sunsama), automation (Make.com vs n8n).
- **LaTeX / Typst typesetting** advocacy. [chat 2026-03-09]
- **Hebrew TTS** — pronunciation tricks via ElevenLabs V3 +
  Ashkenazi-tuned input.
