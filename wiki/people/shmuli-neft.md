# Shmuli Neft

Author of the chat's most-shared writing-style system prompt — the
anti-AI style sheet.

## What he contributed

[chat 2025-08-04]: shared a detailed 15+ paragraph system prompt for
making AI-written text sound human. Bans em-dashes, "leverage,"
"synergy," "tapestry," "delve," "in conclusion." Sets Flesch reading
score ≥ 80. Active voice only. Plain English. Calm confidence, not
salesy.

Full text in [[../resources/master-prompts]] under the *"Shmuli Neft's

## Why it stuck

The prompt is concrete: a long list of banned words, banned phrases,
and structural rules. It produces letters that *don't sound like
ChatGPT*. Many shluchim use it as the first system message in any
serious writing chat.

Other contributions in the chat include ChatGPT-agent experiments
