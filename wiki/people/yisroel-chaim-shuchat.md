# Yisroel Chaim Shuchat

The chat's resident ChabadOne integration expert and prompt curator.
Runs **ShliachFlow.com** for custom shliach websites; deeply involved
in Shluchim Exchange agents.

## Areas of expertise

- **ChabadOne integration.** Originator of the *Claude artifact + iframe*
  workaround [chat 2025-09-09] that lets shluchim publish HTML5 inside
  ChabadOne's XHTML 1.0 environment. See
  [[../topics/chabadone-integration]].
- **Custom-GPT design** for repeated shliach workflows.
- **Prompt-of-prompt workflow** — ask one AI to write a prompt for
  another, including brand colors, layout, content guidelines.
  [chat 2025-11-20]
- **Holiday minisite builder pattern** — Pesach, Purim, High Holidays
  minisites built with AI + Manus.im + ChabadOne. Examples:
  `jewishpedro.org/.../Passover-Minisite.htm`,
  `.../Purim-Minisite.htm`.
- **Tally.so + Stripe + MCP** — for forms that ChabadOne can't do
  natively. [chat 2026-05-15]
