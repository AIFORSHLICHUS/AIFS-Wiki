# Yossi Yaffe

Builder of [[../tools/learningtanach-org]], the chat's loudest voice
on **AI Torah accuracy**, and the author of the in-progress **9
principles for AI Torah citation**.

## Things he's built

- **learningtanach.org** — interactive Tanach with image-heavy Megillas
  Esther reader. 100+ hours on images and 3D reconstructions. ~7,000
  visitors in 2 days post-Purim 2026.
- **Tzvi-to-Tzadik** (`tzvi-to-tzadik.lovable.app`) — grandfather's
  poems gifted to the Rebbe, AI-powered source exploration.
  [chat 2026-03-29]
- **Rashi Roots Map / Atlas of the Sages** (`rashi-roots-map.lovable.app`) —
  intellectual-history timeline. [chat 2026-01-23]
- **Book of Esther** (`bookofesther.lovable.app`) — live maps +
  meforshim translation.
- **Kiddush HaChodesh** (`kiddushhachodesh.lovable.app`) — Rambam
  Kiddush HaChodesh app.

## The 9 Principles document

[chat 2026-02-23]:

> *"I'm working on a set of principles and detailed rules — written in
> plain language, not code — that you paste into whatever tool you
> already use. No downloads, no new apps. You paste it once and the
> tool should handle Torah texts more carefully. The rules cover
> search methods, citation formats, verification steps, and
> domain-specific instructions for different types of texts."*

Treated by the community as the closest thing to a *system prompt
for AI Torah honesty*. Still iterating as of 2026-05.

## The learningtanach.org post-mortem

[chat 2026-03-04], one of the most-shared artifacts in the chat:

> *"I kept adding pieces using AI tools. Each addition made the
> codebase more fragile. By the end, I had six different timing
> systems contradicting each other."*

Yossi posted his lessons publicly — restart chats, refactor before
adding features, don't release vibe-coded apps with login
functionality. Became a reference for the [[../themes/vibe-coding]]
discipline.

## Bake-offs

[chat 2026-04-21]: ran a major bake-off — Claude Opus 4.7, Sonnet,
Gemini 2.5 Pro and Flash — for Yiddish + Hebrew OCR.

> *"Yiddish and Hebrew is not the same thing."*

His results contributed to the chat's understanding that Yiddish
remains harder than Hebrew and requires specialized models.

## Editorial positions

- **Push limits responsibly.** Yossi's signature: ship, fail in
  public, document the lessons.
- **AI for Torah needs explicit constraints, not blind trust.**
- **Bake off multiple models** for the specific task.

## Style

Learner-in-public. Transparent about failures and the costs
(*"Was it worth 100 hours? I'd do it again."*). High-effort builder
who treats every shipped app as a learning artifact.
