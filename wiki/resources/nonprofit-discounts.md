# Discounts, Free Trials, and Hacks

What the community has documented as ways to lower AI costs. Verify
before relying — offers change.

See [[../themes/paid-vs-free]] for the broader debate.

## Still working (as of 2026-05)

### Perplexity Pro — 1 year free via Samsung Galaxy Store

> *"Valuable hack: if you have a Samsung phone, download Perplexity on
> your phone via Galaxy Store and make an account, you automatically
> get a Pro account for one year, free of charge. You can then access
> that account from anywhere."*
> — [chat 2025-08-04, Rabbi Zalman Abraham]

### Gemini — 4 months free

`g.co/g1referral/ZAPZU83S` — shared by Mendy Shishler 2025-10-20.

### Google AI Studio — free

`aistudio.google.com` with $300 free credit for new API users. Lets
you use Gemini Pro / Nano Banana via the API for free at the rate
limits. Also has a vibe-coding canvas.

### Google Workspace Nonprofit — bundled Gemini

If your Chabad House qualifies (501(c)(3)) you get Gemini for
Workspace plus the storage tier. Caveat: adding Gemini sometimes
*downgrades* the shared-drive tier. Talk to your admin first.
[chat 2025-11, Ari Herson?]

### Gemini Plus — $6/month

The new cheap tier (released ~2025-11). Less generous than Pro.

### ChatGPT Go — $8/month

Middle tier between free and Plus. Released 2026-02-09. Sufficient
for most shluchim who use AI a couple of times a day.

### ChatGPT for Teachers — free through 2027

`openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-teachers/`

Qualifying educators (including yeshiva/day-school staff in some
cases) get free access through 2027.

### Codex for Open Source — 6 months free ChatGPT Pro

`openai.com/form/codex-for-oss` — for open-source developers.
[chat 2026-03 week review]

### Claude for Nonprofits — 75% off (5-seat minimum)

`claude.com/solutions/nonprofits` — 5 × $20 × 0.25 = $40/month total
for a 5-seat plan. [chat 2025-12-02]

### Anthropic Skilljar — free training

`anthropic.skilljar.com` — Anthropic's free Claude certification
courses. Useful when on-boarding a Chabad-House team.

### Amex × ChatGPT Business hack

[chat 2026-05-19, Dan's Deals via Mendy Shishler]:

> to 4 years. Perfect for free service after $300 annual Amex

`dansdeals.com/credit-cards/hurry-get-2-chatgpt-business-seats-for-the-price-of-1-for-up-to-4-years-perfect-for-free-service-after-300-annual-amex-credit/`

[chat 2026-02-16, Meir Sudak] distributed $25 Lovable gift cards.

### Turboscribe — 3 free transcriptions/day

`turboscribe.ai` — bulk plan $20/month. Best value the chat has

## Hard to qualify

- **OpenAI for Nonprofits** (Goodstack-verified, 20% off) — academic,
  medical, governmental institutions excluded. [chat 2026-01-13,
  Ari Kievman]

## Tools designed to compound savings

- **OpenAI Codex for OSS** (above) + **Claude for Nonprofits** (above)
  = stack across model providers.
- **Poe AI** — bundles Claude, GPT, Gemini under one subscription if
  you want one bill. API-based, lacks some native-app features.
  [chat 2025-08]
- **LMArena.ai** — side-by-side LLM comparisons, free.
  [chat 2026-01-09, +972 50-636-0770]

## Caveats

- Free tiers train on your data unless you opt out. Several
  warnings: [chat 2025-12-27, Meir Sudak], [chat 2026-05-07].
- Workspace admins can see Gemini chats on managed accounts — users
  can't easily delete. [chat 2026-05-07, +1 773-218-1108]
