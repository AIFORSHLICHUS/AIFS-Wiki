# The Model Leapfrog

The single most repeated genre of message in the chat is: *"Which model
is best right now?"* And the second most repeated is: *"It changed again
this week."* The community has come to call this the **AI leapfrog**.

[external: as of mid-2026, the competitive frontier has moved beyond
what the chat tracked. Claude Opus 4, GPT-5, and Gemini 2.5 are now
the main contenders at the top tier. The leapfrog pattern itself
continues — test this week, not last month. — external: knowledge
cutoff ~mid-2026]

## The pattern

Every few months, one of ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini ships an update
that briefly makes it the obvious choice, and the chat shifts. Three
or four months later, another vendor catches up and the chat shifts
again. Loyalty doesn't compound; the only durable answer is *test it
this week*.

## What the group actually settled on

For everyday shlichus work, by **May 2026**, the rough consensus:

- **ChatGPT** — easiest on-ramp; best mobile; best image generation
  after the GPT 5.2 image model. New shluchim should start here.
  [[../tools/chatgpt]]
- **Claude** — best for "serious desktop work": code, long documents,
  spreadsheets, organized shiurim, formatted curricula. Worst mobile
  experience. Now usable on the Pro plan after the SpaceX deal.
  [[../tools/claude]]
- **Gemini** — best Google-Workspace integration; best image work
  (Nano Banana Pro); strong vision; *Pro* only, never Flash. Quality
  perceived as volatile through Q1 2026. [[../tools/gemini]]
- **Perplexity** — for research with citations; weaker for nuance.
  [[../tools/perplexity]]
- **Grok** — fewer "woke" guardrails, useful when other models refuse
  legitimate prompts; some image strengths. [[../tools/grok]]
- **Qwen** — surprise winner for pure Hebrew/Yiddish translation
  (provides etymology, shoresh, word-by-word transliteration).
  [chat 2026-04-16, +33 6 51 82 35 18]

> *"Pay for one and use it for everything."*

A poll on 2026-04-16 asked what's most important in a model.
Top answers:

1. *Remembers and understands your context (you, your community, etc.)* — 50 votes
2. *Being able to trust it (fewer hallucinations)* — 39 votes
3. *Connects well to your tools (CRM, email, drive)* — 23 votes
4. *One tool that does all your AI needs so you don't juggle* — 24 votes
5. *Sounds more human (not like AI)* — 19 votes

Notice how low *coding/agentic* (6 votes) ranks — this is a non-engineer
community using AI in production.

## Tensions

- **"Should we even learn AI?"** Tools change so fast that prompting
  itself is throwaway skill; just use agents. Counter: prompting is
  tool-agnostic and durable. Unresolved. [chat 2026-02-13]
- **Trust vs. speed.** Claude slow but rigorous; Gemini fast but
  hallucinates; ChatGPT in the middle. The group routes by use case.
- **Cost vs. utility.** $20/mo Plus considered baseline by ~65% of
  members (poll, 24 yes / 13 no, [chat 2025-09-17]). Power users hit
  Claude Max ($100) and Manus.im ($200) too.
