# Paid vs. Free

Whether the $20/month is worth it. Whether nonprofit discounts apply.
Which free trials are still alive. A standing debate.

## The default recommendation

> *"Every shliach must have at least one paid AI plan. $20 is באין ערוך."*
> — [chat 2025-09-17]

Poll the same day: **24 paid, 13 free-only.** Majority but not consensus.

## The economic argument

- Free ChatGPT throttles at ~5 substantive queries/day and limits
  uploads. Sufficient for casual use; insufficient for any flyer batch,
  data cleanup, or file-heavy workflow.
- Plus ($20) lifts the wall enough that most shluchim cease noticing it.
- Pro / Max / Ultra ($100–$200) earn their keep only for true power
  users (Manus.im agent flows, Claude Code long sessions, deep
  research, Nano Banana Pro at scale).

## The counter-position

> *"Don't feel pressured."* — [chat 2025-09-17] —
> discontinued paid plan due to low usage; works fine on free with
> occasional Perplexity for research.

The argument: if you're not yet generating real flyers, real letters,
real spreadsheets *daily*, $20 is wasted money. Start free, upgrade
*when you hit the wall*.

> *"Once you understand the limits, you'll upgrade naturally."*
> — [chat 2025-09-17] (paraphrased).

## What burns cost

Usage that turns Pro plans into Max plans into "I'm spending $300/month":

- **Claude Code / Cursor for vibe-coding.** Token-heavy. Cursor users
  routinely reported $300–$500/mo. [chat 2026-02-22]
- **Deep Research workflows** in Gemini / ChatGPT.
- **Long context windows.** Long shiur transcripts, multi-document
  research notebooks.
- **Agent runs.** [[../tools/lovable]], Manus.im, Claude Dispatch /
  Cowork — minutes of work = lots of tokens.

## The frugal alternative stack

If you're committed to spending little:

- **Google AI Studio** for free high-token Gemini access (better than
  Gemini app for some tasks). [chat 2025-11-21]
- **Google Workspace Nonprofit** if your Chabad House qualifies — gets
  you Gemini and the storage tier.
- **Free Hebrew transcription**: ivrit.ai (`transcribe.ivrit.ai`).
- **Perplexity Pro** via Samsung Galaxy Store hack.
- **Turboscribe** free tier (3 transcripts/day).
- **Lovable / Base44** free credits for tiny prototypes; graduate to
  Claude Code (free with Claude Pro) for anything serious.
