# Canva

Not an AI tool per se, but **the final step in nearly every
[[../topics/flyer-design]] workflow**, because Canva is where you can
actually edit text after AI generated the image.

## Three things the chat uses it for

1. **Text editing on AI-generated images.** AI struggles with text;
   strip it out, paste the visual into Canva, type the text in a font
   you control.
2. **Magic Layers** — released early 2026. Decomposes a flat AI image
   into separate editable elements. *"Game-changer for editing
   AI-generated ads."* [chat 2026-03-08]. Available on Pro accounts.
3. **Canva AI itself** — built-in generative design. Connectors to
   ChatGPT and Claude via MCP.

## The standard pattern (combined with AI)

[chat 2026-02-02]:

> *"I have success with GPT (for the content) sending it directly to
> Canva AI using connectors. You may need to edit some of the words or
> images of the slideshow. Canva AI (once it receives it) takes some
> liberties. But it gives you a very good base to start with."*

[chat 2025-11-20] — the standard ChatGPT-prompts-Canva flow:

> *"I tell ChatGPT what information needs to be on the flyer and it
> puts together a prompt for me (it also includes my brand colors). I
> paste that into Canva AI, and it gives me a few nice options to
> edit. If you use Gemini only to create the background and then add
> the text in Canva, that can come out quite nice."*

## Strengths

- Mature, familiar tool — shluchim were using it before AI.
- Templates &amp; brand kits work reliably.
- Magic Layers + Affinity (Photoshop competitor, Canva-owned) for power
  use.
- Nonprofit pricing available.

## Weaknesses

- Canva AI generation alone is *"useless"* per
  [chat 2026-02-11] and *"awful"* per
  [chat 2025-11-24].
- Mobile less capable than desktop.
- Takes liberties with words/images that don't match your spec.

## MCP / Connector integration

[chat 2026-03-25] asked about *downloading Canva into
Claude*. Clarified: it's an **MCP integration**, not a download. Claude
can drive Canva through MCP, but you still log into Canva to see
results. Genuinely useful for batch flyer work.

## Affinity (Canva's Photoshop alternative)

[chat 2026-05-13]: *"Canva Pro subscription can use
more powerful upscale (and other AI) features on Affinity."* Affinity
is Canva-owned; included with Canva Pro. Useful for upscaling logos
and post-flyer touch-up.
