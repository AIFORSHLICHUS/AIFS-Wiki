## Why this matters

The chat is a building community as much as a discussion community.
Most of these shipped between November 2025 and May 2026 — a window
when AI app-builders crossed a usability threshold. The pattern that
keeps working: **small problem, in-browser, no login, no payments,
narrow corpus.**
