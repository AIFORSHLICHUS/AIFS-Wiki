# Qwen

Alibaba's open LLM. The chat's **surprise winner** for pure Hebrew /
Yiddish translation work.

## What it is

`chat.qwen.ai` — multilingual LLM from Alibaba. Free chat access.
Strong on translation tasks, especially Semitic languages.

## The thread that put it on the map

[chat 2026-04-16, +33 6 51 82 35 18], after testing eight different
LLMs:

> *"I made many tests over 8 different LLMs and for pure translation
> I found Qwen to be ahead of any other LLM for pure translation
> (pesoukim, or any Hebrew text). He doesn't just give the translation
> — he also provides the etymology, the root (shoresh), occurrences,
> and a word-by-word transliteration. It's very practical. He also
> gives the context of the verse."*

## What the chat uses it for

- **Hebrew translation with grammatical metadata** — shoresh,
  etymology, transliteration.
- **Pasuk-by-pasuk parsing** for shiur prep.
- **Word-by-word Hebrew/English breakdown** for *baalei-tefillah*
  references.

## Strengths

- Pure-translation accuracy ahead of Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini.
- Free.
- Provides grammatical analysis without being asked.

## Weaknesses

- Not a general-purpose chat assistant.
- UX less polished than the big three.
- English writing quality nowhere near Claude/ChatGPT.

## When to use it

When the *core question* is "translate this Hebrew correctly and tell
me what each word means." For everything else, default to
[[claude]] / [[chatgpt]] / [[gemini]].
