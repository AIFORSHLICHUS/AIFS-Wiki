# SinaiGPT

The chat's *first* AI artifact — a Torah-focused custom GPT made by
[[../people/mendy-shishler]] before the group existed.

## Context

[chat 2025-07-30, Mendy Shishler]: *"https://tinyurl.com/SinaiGPT.
Made this ages ago, a guy just reminded me of it."*

Shared two days after the group's founding. Marked the "we've been
thinking about AI for Torah for a while now" moment that turned the
chat from a Meta-AI joke into a working community.

## Status

Functional but predates the group's more careful patterns for
preventing Torah hallucination. By 2026 the community had moved on to
[[notebooklm]] with vetted source corpora, [[maamorim-app]], and
the in-progress [[dicta-mekorotai]].

SinaiGPT remains a useful reference for *what a Chabad Torah GPT
looks like* — historically. See [[../themes/ai-and-torah-accuracy]]
for why custom-GPT-on-recall has hallucination limits.
